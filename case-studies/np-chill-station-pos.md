## Overview

A point-of-sale and operations application designed for a café environment with ordering, kitchen tickets, and billing.

## Key capabilities

- Sales billing and kitchen order ticket (KOT) workflow
- AC and non-AC seating price configurations
- Custom sale items and separate purchase entry
- Supplier and customer balances
- Bill lookup, reprint, correction, and staff permissions
- Employee attendance and payment-out record keeping

## Representative workflow

Select seating section → add items → send kitchen ticket → maintain pending order → finalize bill on payment.

## Design considerations

- Clear, responsive screens for daily operational work
- Accessible navigation and fast lookup of records
- Administrative controls appropriate to sensitive business data
- Traceable corrections and a focus on reducing manual steps
