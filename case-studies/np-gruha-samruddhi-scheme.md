## Overview

A business application for administering a 12-month customer savings scheme for furniture, electronics, and appliances.

## Key capabilities

- Customer onboarding and unique customer identification
- Five monthly contribution plan options and installment tracking
- Payment records, receipts, and correction workflows
- Maturity benefit status and redemption management
- Role-based staff access, admin controls, and audit history
- Operational reports and business-facing workflows

## Representative workflow

Onboard customer → select plan → record monthly installments → issue receipt → review maturity benefit → manage redemption.

## Design considerations

- Clear, responsive screens for daily operational work
- Accessible navigation and fast lookup of records
- Administrative controls appropriate to sensitive business data
- Traceable corrections and a focus on reducing manual steps
