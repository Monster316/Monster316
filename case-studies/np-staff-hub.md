## Overview

A workforce management application designed to help staff and managers review attendance and coordinate leave and schedule exceptions, with biometric attendance integration as a project requirement.

## Key capabilities

- Employee sign-in and attendance history views
- Late-arrival and schedule-exception explanations
- Leave application with manager approval or rejection
- Weekly-off request and approval process
- Team chat and staff communication requirements
- Login/logout notifications and access-aware administration

## Representative workflow

Attendance device logs punch → app sync layer processes attendance → employee views status → manager reviews exceptions and leave.

## Design considerations

- Clear, responsive screens for daily operational work
- Accessible navigation and fast lookup of records
- Administrative controls appropriate to sensitive business data
- Traceable corrections and a focus on reducing manual steps
